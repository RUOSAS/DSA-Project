## Project Topic: E Commerce Sales Analysis

### Project Overview

Sales data analysis is the process of collecting, organizing, examining, and interpreting sales information to understand business performance, identify trends, and make better business decisions.

This document helps a business understand what is selling, how much revenue is being generated, who the customers are, and how sales can be improved.

### Data Sources

The primary data sources are the places or systems from which sales information is collected for analysis. They provide the raw information needed to understand sales performance, customer behaviour, revenue, and business growth.
